# The goal, mapped against who we're actually selling against

Researched 2026-08-25 from public reviews and pricing. Every gap below is one a
customer of theirs complains about today — not a feature we invented a need for.

---

## Where they are

### Apiiro — enterprise, deep, closed

**What they're strong at:** Risk Graph™ contextualising third-party alerts,
reachability analysis, code-to-runtime matching of APIs/containers back to
source. They claim triage-time reduction of 95%.

**What their customers complain about**, and it is a short, specific list:

| Complaint | What it means for us |
|---|---|
| **Performance problems on on-premises deployments** | We are on-prem/air-gap native. This is their weakest flank. |
| **Wants self-hosted Git servers beyond GitHub** | We are ingest-first — we take any scanner's output and never need repo access at all. |
| **Manual triage still required; limited automatic risk distinction** | Our exploitability verdict is the whole product, and it labels its own confidence. |
| **Complex when AppSec maturity is low; setup and policy tuning needed** | Eight flows, one job each. |
| **Enterprise sales only, opaque pricing** | A published price is itself a differentiator against them. |

### Aikido — SMB, broad, capped

**Pricing (public):** free for 2 users, then **$350 / $700 / $1,050 per month**
for 10 users, up to ~$8,000. Pentests bill separately from **$4,000**.

**What they're strong at:** 15+ scanners in one place, auto-filtering irrelevant
findings, one-click remediation PRs, genuinely developer-friendly.

**Their limits, which are structural rather than accidental:** no built-in
security training, limited retention of historic container scan records,
fair-usage repo caps, free repos rescan only every 3 days, AI autofix capped at
10 per month. Their reviewer base is overwhelmingly small business and startup.

**They are not our competitor.** They win the bottom of the market on price and
breadth. Fighting them there is a losing trade.

## Where we actually win

Ranked by how hard it is for them to copy, and each already verified in
production (see `docs/COMPETITIVE_POSITION_2026-08-25.md`).

**1. Evidence an auditor can verify without us.** Bundles are sealed, signed
RSA-SHA256 over a content hash, and checkable offline with
`scripts/verify_evidence_bundle.py` — no network, no FixOps, no account. Tamper
is provably detected. *Neither competitor has this.* Their evidence is only as
trustworthy as their own console, and for a regulated buyer that is the whole
argument.

**2. The customer's own risk model.** Apiiro's graph is theirs. A tenant here
declares its own entity types and correlation rules, and rules are refused
(HTTP 400) if they try to assert a measurement. This is a direct answer to
"closed risk graph", and it is already live.

**3. Air-gapped operation.** Their loudest on-prem complaint is *performance*.
Ours is a first-class deployment mode with a fail-closed profile. This is where
the SCIF and defence market lives, and neither of them is there.

**4. A decision, not a list — with its confidence stated.** Reachability ×
exploit evidence → act_now / schedule / watch / defer, each carrying whether
the evidence was *measured* (KEV, real EPSS) or *estimated*. Apiiro's own
customers say manual triage remains. Saying "act now, but on a guess" is the
honesty that makes automated triage trustworthy.

**5. Ingest-first.** We never need repo access. Apiiro customers asking for
self-hosted Git support are describing a problem we do not have.

## What we still owe before this is sellable

Each of these is a Multica card. Ordered by what blocks a sale.

| # | Task | Why it blocks | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | Re-measure the dead-screen count against the fixed build | The first thing an evaluator does is click everything. It is how the P0 was found. | a current number, not the stale ~80 |
| G2 | Browser-verify the six unverified console screens | A screen that renders an empty panel reads as "this product does nothing" | each shows real data or an honest empty state |
| G3 | Measure reachability's noise reduction on a real repo | Apiiro quotes 95%. We quote nothing. It is the first question in any bake-off | a published percentage from a real repo |
| G4 | Publish a price | Apiiro's opacity is a complaint; a public number is a wedge | three tiers on a page |
| G5 | Delete what nothing imports | 2.2M LOC, 4.99M repomix tokens. Dead weight slows every future change | repomix token count materially down |
| G6 | One-command air-gapped install | Our strongest flank is unreachable if it takes a week to stand up | `docker compose up` on a disconnected host |
| G7 | Retire evidence-collector and evidence-vault, or wire them | Two unfed silos returning zeros | each wired or removed |
| G8 | Prove the exploitability verdict on a customer-scale repo | It is our core claim and it has only run on small inputs | verdict distribution over 1,000+ findings |
